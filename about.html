Our story

Built by islanders, for island trips

PARAGOS started as a way to book a single ferry ticket without five different apps. It grew into a trip planner for exploring the Philippines.

Why we started

Local knowledge, in one itinerary

PARAGOS was founded in Manila in 2019 by a small group of former ferry operators and hotel managers who kept fielding the same question from travelers: why does island hopping take five separate bookings and three phone calls to confirm?

We built PARAGOS to combine stays, boat and flight connections, and rental cars into a single itinerary, priced upfront and organized around the same local travel details that matter on the ground.

"We wanted a trip planner that actually knows the difference between a habal-habal and a van transfer. That local detail is the whole product."

— Mira Santos, Co-founder

What we stand for

Our values

The principles that guide how we build partnerships and support trips.

Local-first

Every partner stay, boat, and rental car in our network is based in the Philippines.

Transparent pricing

The price you see at search is the price you pay, with the applicable booking costs shown before confirmation.

Real human support

Our support experience is designed around helping travelers when schedules change or plans need adjusting.

Responsible travel

We favor partners with visible conservation and community reinvestment practices.
